I am a Doctor of Engineering student in Electronic Information at Tsinghua University's Institute for Network Sciences and Cyberspace, where I work in the Internet Architecture Laboratory. I am advised by Prof. Han Zhang and Prof. Jilong Wang.

My research focuses on **Internet measurement** and **cyberspace situational awareness**, with an emphasis on **Web PKI observability**. I build Internet-wide measurement and analysis tools for SSL/TLS certificates and study security issues in certificate issuance.

My current work includes an Internet-wide SSL/TLS certificate database that unifies approximately 70 million actively scanned certificates with 1.1 billion Certificate Transparency log certificates. I also build certificate–asset relationship graphs and analysis capabilities for certificate content, forged-certificate detection, and large-scale asset profiling.

Previously, I received dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and Zhejiang University. My undergraduate research spanned programming-assignment error localization, SAT/SMT solvers, and static binary analysis.
